Overview

One Movement.
Two Time Scales.

Some mechanisms are understood immediately. This one takes a little longer.

The tourbillon remains in view. The calendar works beneath the surface. Between them, the movement reveals only what is necessary and leaves the rest to be discovered over time. Only eight pieces will ever be produced.

Display layout

Time and Calendar

The dial separates timekeeping from the calendar displays to improve legibility. Hours and minutes remain central, while the day, date, and month are assigned to dedicated indications, leaving the flying tourbillon fully visible at the lower edge.

Complications

Regulation and Calendar Control

Flying Tourbillon

The Regulating Organ

The balance and escapement are mounted within a cage that completes one rotation every 60 seconds. The cage is supported from below, providing an unobstructed view of the regulating assembly.

Perpetual calendar

Four-Year Programme

The calendar mechanism distinguishes months of 28, 29, 30 and 31 days. It coordinates the date, day, month and leap-year displays through a 48-month programme.

Continuous operation avoids routine correction for short months and leap years until the Gregorian exception in 2100.
