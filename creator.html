01Beginning

The project began with the movement.

Calibre 1 started in April 2024 as an attempt to design a complete mechanical watch movement from first principles.

Rather than modifying an existing calibre, the gear train, winding system, escapement, flying tourbillon, perpetual calendar, plates, bridges, and jewel layout as one connected system.

The result is an hand-wound movement of more than 250 components, combining a one-minute flying tourbillon with a 48-month mechanical calendar programme.

The work

One project. Every layer.

Architecture

The movement was laid out around its complications, with the power path, clearances, component support, and assembly sequence considered together from the first design.

Validation

Gear ratios, torque, tolerance stacks, endshake, and component interaction were evaluated through calculation, CAD review, and enlarged 6× physical prototypes.

Presentation

The mechanical work was carried through to the visual identity, technical renders, exploded animation, product specifications, and website for the completed calibre.

Approach

Engineering before decoration.

Every visible decision begins with a mechanical reason.

The movement is treated as both a machine and an arrangement of space. Structure, service access, power transfer, and legibility determine the architecture first; finishing is then used to clarify the result rather than disguise it.
